Privacy Policy

At HostelFix, we value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal information. This detailed Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

1 Information We Collect

We collect information designed to provide seamless hostel issue tracking and management across Students, Wardens, and Administrators:

👤 Personal Info

Name, email address, institutional role, and hostel details via Firebase Authentication.

🛠️ Issue Data

Maintenance reports, descriptions, status logs, and optional uploaded photos.

🔔 Technical Data

Device identifiers and push notification tokens (FCM) for real-time status alerts.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate users securely and enforce role-based access control (Student, Warden, Admin).
  • To facilitate efficient reporting, tracking, assignment, and resolution of campus hostel issues.
  • To dispatch essential push notifications regarding complaint status updates and announcements.

3 Third-Party Services & Firebase

HostelFix integrates securely with Google Firebase for backend infrastructure, including Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database / Firestore, Firebase Storage, and Cloud Messaging. All data transmitted is protected under Google’s robust security standards. You can check Google's privacy policy here.

4 Data Security & Retention

We employ industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS in transit) to protect your data. Information is retained only as long as necessary to provide our services. You can request account termination and data deletion at any time by contacting our support team.

5 Children's Privacy

Our application is intended exclusively for college and university students and residential staff. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13.
